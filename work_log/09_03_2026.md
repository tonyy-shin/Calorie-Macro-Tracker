# 09/03/2026

## Phase 3 — Gold layer (started)

- Investigated barcode duplication across silver tables before writing
  the gold merge. Found duplicates break down into three categories:
  - 381,197 barcodes duplicated *within* USDA alone (same product
    resubmitted under multiple fdc_ids over time)
  - 60 barcodes duplicated *within* OFF alone (already known from
    silver work)
  - 29,625 barcodes present in *both* USDA and OFF — genuine
    cross-source collisions
- Decided dedup strategy: within each source, rank duplicate rows by
  macro completeness (count of non-null kcal/protein_g/carbs_g/fat_g)
  descending, tiebreak by loaded_at descending, keep rank 1 via
  ROW_NUMBER() + PARTITION BY barcode. Barcodeless rows bypass ranking
  entirely and pass through via UNION ALL, since PARTITION BY would 
  incorrectly group all NULLs together otherwise.
- For the 29,625 cross-source collisions: USDA wins (government
  curated data preferred over crowd sourced OFF).
- Wrote and ran `usda_food_gold.sql` — dedups usda_food_silver by the
  above rule. Verified: 553,100 total rows, 465,110 distinct barcodes,
  465,110 barcode-populated rows (exact match — confirms one row per
  barcode), 87,990 barcode-less passthrough rows.
